# What Blockaid actually did — and why it is not copyable

I previously said Blockaid "published research that made them the obvious pick,
then got embedded." **That was wrong.** The timeline says the opposite.

## The actual sequence

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Sept 2022** | Founded by Ido Ben-Natan and Raz Niv — both from **Unit 8200** (Israeli military cyber intelligence) and the cyber R&D team at the **Office of the Prime Minister of Israel**. |
| **Late 2022** | **$6M seed within ~3 months of founding** — Sequoia, Greylock, **Cyberstarts**. |
| **2023** | ~13 months in **stealth**, building integrations. A MetaMask experimental scam-warning feature ships with OpenSea during this period. |
| **Oct 2023** | Emerge from stealth **already holding MetaMask, OpenSea, Rainbow and Zerion** — announced simultaneously with a **$27M Series A** (Ribbit, Variant). |
| **2025** | **$50M Series B**; ~$83M total. |

They did not publish their way in. **They came out of stealth with the logos
already signed.**

## The mechanism was the investor, not the marketing

**Cyberstarts** is not a generic seed fund. Its explicit model — the **Sunrise
program** — is matching portfolio startups to buyers: an adviser network of CISOs
who take introductions to the fund's three or four new investments each year.
Reporting names Chipotle, JLL and Takeda as companies whose security leaders
participated and which then signed contracts with Cyberstarts-backed startups.

Note for accuracy: Cyberstarts **stopped compensating those CISOs in June**, after
Calcalist reporting raised conflict-of-interest concerns over payments reported up
to $250,000. So this is not merely un-copyable — part of it was retired under
ethical criticism and should not be imitated.

## The honest read

Blockaid's distribution came from three things a solo operator cannot buy:

1. **Pedigree.** Unit 8200 plus the PM's Office is the most fundable credential in
   security. It closed a seed round in roughly ninety days.
2. **A fund engineered for introductions.** The design-partner problem — the
   hardest part of enterprise security sales — was solved by the cap table.
3. **Capital to stay quiet.** $6M bought thirteen months of building integrations
   with no revenue and no need to be noticed.

There is no tactic hidden in there. Asking "how did they get MetaMask" has the
answer "their investors introduced them, and their CVs made the meeting happen."

## What IS structurally transferable

Three things, none of them a growth hack:

1. **Design partners before announcement, not customers after.** Blockaid shipped a
   real feature inside MetaMask *while still in stealth*. The generalisable form:
   get one real integration working with one real counterparty before telling
   anyone anything. Slower without intros — but it is the actual sequence, and it
   is the opposite of publishing first.
2. **They picked a category with a screaming victim.** Wallet drainers stole
   identifiable money from identifiable users, and MetaMask owned the
   complaint. "An agent overpaid for an API" has no equivalent victim yet — nobody
   is angry, so nobody is buying. That is a **timing** difference, not a tactical
   one, and it is the single biggest reason this is harder now than it was for
   them in 2022.
3. **They needed to be TOLD what was malicious; we can MEASURE it.** Blockaid's
   early signal came from partners reporting scams. The corpus here — 13,901
   probed servers, 127,403 tool definitions, a monthly diff — finds things without
   anyone reporting them. That is the one asymmetry running in our favour, and it
   is worth more than any of their tactics.

## What this changes about the plan

- **Stop treating "publish research" as the proven path.** It was not their path. It
  may still be the right one here, but it should be argued on its own merits, not
  on a false precedent I asserted earlier.
- **The Blockaid email is worth less as a distribution play than I implied**, and
  worth exactly as much as I said for the other reason: they can tell us in one
  line whether the payment-sanity gap is real.
- **The design-partner sequence is the transferable part.** One working integration
  with one real counterparty beats five cold emails, and it is still entirely
  behind a computer.
